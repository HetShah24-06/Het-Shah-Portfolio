About

I like the part of an app nobody sees: the routes, the data model, the validation that stops bad input before it reaches the database. Most of my work is building REST APIs with Node.js and Express and designing schemas in PostgreSQL and MongoDB, then wiring them into React and Next.js front ends.

Since September 2025 I've worked remotely as a consultant at Void Enterprise, after an internship there. At Humber Polytechnic I led a three-person Agile team on a capstone for Credits Financial Inc. and finished my Postgraduate Certificate on the Dean's Honours List.

I've also built outside JavaScript: a JavaFX healthcare app, a C#/.NET cafe system, and mobile apps in Swift and Java.
